## 场景记忆法

### 🏠 家中场景
bed → I get out of **bed**.
brush → I brush my hair with a **brush**.
cup → I drink from a **cup**.
door → I open the **door**.
kettle → The **kettle** is boiling.

### 🌳 户外场景
tree → The **tree** has green leaves.
flower → A red **flower** in the garden.
bird → A **bird** on the roof.
rain → The **rain** comes down.
cloud → A dark **cloud** in the sky.

### 🏙️ 城市场景
street → Walk down the **street**.
school → The **school** is near my house.
train → I take the **train** to work.
bridge → Go over the **bridge**.
hospital → The **hospital** is on the north side.
