# 每周详细计划

## Week 1 — Operations（100词）

### 每日分配

| 天 | 新词 | 数量 | 重点 |
|----|------|------|------|
| Mon | 动词18 + 代词4 | 22 | come/get/give/go/keep/let/make/put/seem/take/be/do/have/say/see/send/may/will + I/he/you/who |
| Tue | 限定词11 + 介词前10 | 21 | a/the/all/any/every/no/other/some/such/that/this + about/across/after/against/among/at/before/between/by/down |
| Wed | 介词后10 + 连词10 | 20 | from/in/off/on/over/through/to/under/up/with + as/for/of/till/and/because/but/or/if/though |
| Thu | 副词前15 | 15 | how/when/where/why/again/ever/far/forward/here/near/now/out/still/then/there |
| Fri | 副词后10 + 方位4 + 其他3 | 17 | together/well/almost/enough/even/little/much/not/only/quite/so/very/tomorrow/yesterday + north/south/east/west + please/yes |

### 输出任务
- Mon: 5句动词日记
- Tue: 用介词描述位置关系
- Wed: 用连词组合句子
- Thu: 用副词描述时间/方式
- Fri: 综合造句
- Sat: 10句综合日记
- Sun: 复习薄弱词
