## 反义词对例句

1. The baby is **awake**. ↔ The baby is **asleep**.
2. This is **bad** food. ↔ This is **good** food.
3. The medicine is **bitter**. ↔ The sugar is **sweet**.
4. The night is **dark**. ↔ The day is **light**.
5. This road is **narrow**. ↔ That road is **wide**.
6. The wall is **thick**. ↔ The paper is **thin**.
7. The ice is **cold**. ↔ The fire is **hot**.
8. The bed is **soft**. ↔ The stone is **hard**.
9. My cup is **full**. ↔ His cup is **empty**.
10. The door is **open**. ↔ The door is **shut**.
